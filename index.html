About the event

From visibility to connection.

Four years after the beginning of Woman, Life, Freedom, we come together not only to remember, but also to connect.

This event brings civil-society organizations, activists, professionals, artists, political groups, and members of the wider community into one shared space.

Instead of focusing only on speeches and symbolic presence, the day is designed around direct exchange: meeting organizations, discovering resources, sharing knowledge, contributing skills, and creating possibilities for future cooperation.

01 Remember

Honor those affected by repression and preserve the movement’s memory.

02 Connect

Bring organizations, professionals, artists, and communities together.
